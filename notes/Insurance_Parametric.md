# Insurance Parametric


**Summary**: Parametric and index-based insurance products pay out based on objective triggers rather than assessed losses, offering faster recovery and new approaches to closing the protection gap in climate-exposed communities.
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01.

---

## What is parametric insurance

- [What is Parametric Insurance](https://corporatesolutions.swissre.com/insights/knowledge/what_is_parametric_insurance.html): Parametric (index-based) insurance covers the probability of a loss-causing event rather than indemnifying actual losses, paying out when a pre-defined intensity threshold is met as measured by an objective parameter. #parametric #index-based #threshold #objective-trigger #payout

## Parametric insurance in practice

- *Capitol Gains podcast — Climate, Insurance and Risk Management with Carolyn Kousky*: Parametric insurance pays out automatically based on triggers like wind speed or rainfall, offering fast cash, flexibility for non-property losses, and accessibility for renters and small businesses; however it must be paired with broader policy reforms and resilient building codes to be effective. [[Insurance]] [[Insurance_Climate]] #parametric #Carolyn-Kousky #policy-reform #building-codes #resilience #equity

- *Tahoe Donner Wildfire Resilience Insurance*: A parametric policy piloted in Truckee, CA pays a predetermined amount per burned acre across 1,345 insured acres (up to $2.5M), with lower payouts for beneficial low-severity fire; forest treatment resulted in a 39% lower premium and 84% lower deductible, demonstrating nature-based risk reduction can be recognized by insurers. [[Insurance_Fire]] [[Insurance_Nature]] #wildfire #parametric #Tahoe-Donner #forest-management #prescribed-fire #satellite-imagery

- [Floodbase — Parametric Flood Insurance](https://www.floodbase.com/): Floodbase pioneered large-area flood mapping technology that enables a new category of parametric flood insurance, paying out within days based on the magnitude of flooding over a wide area rather than individual property assessments. ## Policy and legislative efforts

- *AB 1236 — Local Climate Insurance Pilot Projects*: California legislation to develop proofs of concept for community-based and parametric insurance mechanisms, aiming to shrink the protection gap in vulnerable communities through pilot projects that pair insurance with risk reduction and nature-based solutions.
