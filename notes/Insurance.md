# Insurance


**Summary**: The state of property insurance in California and beyond under climate-driven wildfire and flood risk, including market stress, the FAIR Plan, insurance reform, community-based and parametric products, and financing resilience.
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01.

## Market stress and insurability

- [Is California becoming Uninsurable](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/is-california-becoming-uninsurable): Kousky examines how the 2017/18 fires stressed California's insurance market; solutions include risk reduction and assistance for lower-income households. #insurance/fire
- [The world is getting riskier WSJ article](https://www.wsj.com/economy/consumers/the-world-is-getting-riskier-americans-dont-want-to-pay-for-it-51901067): WSJ on the insurance breakdown with California as a microcosm of affordability vs. risk. #insurance
- [Is your state becoming uninsurable](https://grist.org/economics/is-your-state-becoming-uninsurable-we-have-the-latest-data/): State-by-state home insurance buckling data; California premiums up 16%. #insurance
- [NRDC an uninsurable country](https://www.nrdc.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/Insurability_and_CC_R_26-02-A_04_locked.pdf): Rob Moore (2026) on the insurability crisis, rising uninsured homes, and needed state leadership. #insurance
- [Insurance canary in the coal mine](https://www.epicenterinsights.com/the-weekly-the-insurance-canary-in-the-coal-mine/): Government shutdown halted NFIP, stalling real estate transactions. #insurance/flood
- [The insurance sector is the canary in the coal mine](https://www.linkedin.com/): Allianz's Günther Thallinger warns of an impending climate credit crisis and uninsurable regions. #insurance/climate
- [The Insurance Signal How Climate Volatility is Repricing Property Markets](https://www.creosyndicate.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026_CREO_The_Insurance_Signal.pdf): Weinberg (2026) on insurance repricing as a market signal and community-based risk pooling. #insurance
- *Is the future insurable — global climate change is shrinking the range of insurability*: Glendon, Kousky & Schaubl (2025) identify technical, economic, and political limits to insurability. #insurance/climate
- [Future of State-Created Insurance Programmes](https://journalofcrr.com/comment/03-01-kousky-friedrich/): Kousky & Friedrich (2025) on residual/state markets of last resort with a table of programs. #insurance/climate
- [Risk Mitigation Approaches Residual Insurance Programs](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/summary-of-risk-mitigation-approaches-adopted-by-residual-insurance-programs): How residual insurance programs adopt risk mitigation tools like premium credits and grants. #insurance
- [Even Without Hurricanes, U.S. Disaster Costs Surpassed $100 Billion Last Year](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/01/08/climate/us-disaster-damage-costs-2025.html): 2025 U.S. disaster costs reached $115B; LA fires $61B, thunderstorms $51B. #insurance
