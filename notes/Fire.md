## Market context

- [California's home insurer of last resort seeks 36% rate hike](https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2025-10-04/californias-home-insurer-of-last-resort-seeks-36-rate-hike-following-january-fires): The California FAIR Plan seeks an average 35.8% rate hike, its largest in years, after billions in January firestorm losses. #FAIR-Plan #rate-hike #insurer-of-last-resort #California #wildfire-losses

- [Fair plan underwrites 10% of CA residential policies](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brianbastian_wildfireinsurance-propertyrisk-wildfirerisk-activity-7444957690755190784-fHxi/): Bloomberg analysis finds 28% of FAIR Plan exposure sits in lower-risk urban areas while enrollment surged 43% in 15 months, signaling adverse selection spreading geographically; AB 1680 (Make It FAIR Act) follows 17 governance failures found at DOI. #FAIR-Plan #adverse-selection #AB-1680 #market-structure #non-renewal

- *Is California becoming Uninsurable* (Insurance for Good, Carolyn Kousky): Market stress began after 2017-18 fires; insurers reduced willingness to insure fire-prone areas, cut policies via non-renewals, forced-place policies by mortgage lenders rose, more residents turned to FAIR Plan, and premiums/deductibles climbed. #insurability #non-renewal #FAIR-Plan #rate-increases #mortgage

- *State of insurance for wildfires* (Alliance, 2025): Report on rising wildfire risk, insurer withdrawals and non-renewals, an affordability crisis hitting low/middle-income households, FAIR Plan reliance, and innovative solutions like AI risk modeling and parametric insurance. #availability #affordability #mitigation #parametric #FAIR-Plan

- [California Insurance Crisis Panel — The Los Angeles Fires](https://capitolweekly.net/special-episode-california-insurance-crisis-the-los-angeles-fires/): Panel with James Gore, Kelsey Szamet, Nancy Wallace, Steve Hawks covers slow permits, widespread underinsurance in Altadena, toxic smoke damage, retraction of federal FEMA funding, and the need to fund existing mitigation programs. #LA-fires #underinsurance #FEMA #recovery #permit

- [NYT Insurance deserting homeowners](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2024/12/18/climate/insurance-non-renewal-climate-crisis.html): Interactive map of non-renewal rates; in some California counties nonrenewals up 500% since 2018; officials ease rate rules in exchange for insurers staying in fire-prone areas, plus fuels treatment/prescribed burns. #non-renewal #Nevada #forest-treatment #prescribed-burn #mitigation

- [Ricardo Lara CA Insurance Commissioner keynote](https://capitolweekly.net/special-episode-california-insurance-crisis-keynote-by-california-insurance-commissioner-ricardo-lara/): Recovered $30M+ for 11,000 wildfire survivors, pushed Eliminate the List Act, formed Claims and Remediation Task Force for toxic smoke; argues rates won't approach cost of being uninsurable. #Ricardo-Lara #insurance-commissioner #smoke-damage #claims #recovery

- *Ricardo Lara proposals for wildfire mitigation and recovery*: Ten sponsored bills include California Safe Homes Act (home hardening grants), Business Insurance Protection Act (non-renewal moratorium extension), Insurance Payment Protection Act (15% cap on adjuster fees), Eliminate the List Act, FAIR Plan Stability Act, and Savings Accounts for Mitigation. #legislation #home-hardening #non-renewal #FAIR-Plan #tax-free-savings

## Home hardening and mitigation

- [Do California insurers reward wildfire resilience?](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/do-ca-insurers-reward-wildfire-resilience): Kousky & You (2025) find that under 2022 CA regulations, mitigation discounts for home hardening and defensible space vary from a few percent to over 40% by insurer; IBHS WPH discounts range below 4% to 39%, and verification methods differ. #premium-discounts #home-hardening #IBHS #defensible-space #mitigation

- [Building wildfire-resistant homes after disasters](https://headwaterseconomics.org/headwaters/building-wildfire-resistant-homes-after-disasters-will-save-billions/): Rebuilding to wildfire-resistant standards can reduce future losses by up to 43% and avoid $43B in LA losses; costs under 10% more than typical construction, and each $1 spent could save $210. #cost-effectiveness #building-codes #wildfire-resistant-housing #economic-losses #mitigation

- *Building wildfire resistant homes will save billions* (Headwaters Economics, 2025): Report finds wildfire-resistant homes are a smart investment; solutions include adopting building codes, requiring insurance coverage for code upgrades, and funding assistance for rebuilding and upgrades. #building-codes #above-code-rebuilding #funding #IBHS-FORTIFIED #grants

- *Tyrolian Tahoe and Wildfire* (LA Times, 2025): Tyrolian Village launched a wildfire defense project (home hardening, fuel reduction, structure hardening) with grants and robotic vegetation management; residents report ~35% average insurance discounts for recommended mitigations. #Tahoe #home-hardening #fuel-reduction #insurance-discount #community

- [Incline Fire Smart Community Pilot](https://www.tahoefund.org/projects/active-projects/incline-fire-smart-community-pilot/): Pilot in Incline Village, with Tahoe Fund and Chris Anthony, aims to make a neighborhood of 228 homes the most fire-ready community in Tahoe via hardening across public lands, utility corridors, and homes. #Incline-Village #home-hardening #pilot #Tahoe #community

- [Incline Wildfire Ready Pilot](https://www.tahoefund.org/news/innovative-pilot-project-to-create-tahoes-most-wildfire-ready-community-launches/): Seven-phase pilot in Tyrolian Village HOA using Vibrant Planet, Fire Aside, BurnBot, and RockRose Risk for baseline modeling, 35-acre vegetation treatment, parcel-level hardening, and risk measurement to inform insurance incentives. #Tahoe #vegetation-treatment #risk-modeling #home-hardening #insurance-incentives

- [RockRose Risk and Homeowners](https://www.linkedin.com/): Insurer-recognized, documented mitigation (Zone 0 defensible space, fine mesh ember screens, certified projects) lets some property owners keep coverage and cut premiums up to 30%; documentation is key for underwriters. #mitigation #home-hardening #defensible-space #documentation #premium-discount

- [Rockrose Risk Insurance](https://www.rockroserisk.com/): Company helps mitigate wildfire risk and get insured for less; offers discounts for mitigation, and for uninsurable properties helps build small self-insurance companies. #mitigation #self-insurance #discounts #uninsurable #underwriting

- [Primary Drivers of Fire Risk to Structures (FRAP)](https://34c031f8-c9fd-4018-8c5a-4159cdff6b0d-cdn-endpoint.azureedge.net/-/media/calfire-website/what-we-do/fire-resource-assessment-program---frap/webinars-and-events/frap-webinar-gollner_zamanialaei_purnomo-spring2025.pdf): FRAP talk isolating drivers of structure loss in the WUI: structure separation, flame length, ember exposure, building features (hardening), defensible space/Zone 0, and year built; introduces WU-E model improving on HAMADA. #FRAP #WUI #embers #defensible-space #home-hardening #fire-modeling

## Prescribed fire and good fire

- [Managing risk from good fire](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/managing-risk-from-good-fire) (Jenna Knobloch, 2025): Prescribed fire liability insurance is becoming harder to obtain and costlier; solutions include new business models (Conservation United), workforce training, state/federal liability funds, and clarifying liability standards. #prescribed-fire #liability-insurance #good-fire #liability-fund #Conservation-United

- [Good fire II — Karuk Tribe](https://karuktribeclimatechangeprojects.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/good-fire-ii-march-2024.pdf): Report on Indigenous and good fire practices where most insurance addressed is liability for prescribed (Rx) fire. #prescribed-fire #liability #good-fire #Indigenous-burning #Rx-fire

- *Dave Jones at Jt. Institute 7-10-25* (UCB state contract meeting): Biomass utilizers need long-term (>10 yr) feedstock contracts but insurance is unavailable to de-risk them; parametrics are written for only 1-3 years and markets won't cover 5-year feedstock; proposes a claims/guaranty fund for long-term contracts. #adaptive-insurance #parametric #biomass-feedstock #claims-fund #Dave-Jones

- *Wildfire Risk Reduction in a Changing Climate* (Swiss Re/Liberty Mutual, 2025): Report summarizes the climate-nature nexus to wildfire, showcases where ecosystem services reduce fire hazard, identifies barriers to implementation (e.g., prescribed fire), and proposes policy, financial and research tools. #ecosystem-services #prescribed-fire #climate-change #risk-reduction #nature-based

## Utilities

- *Edison downgrade: wildfire risks become credit risk* (Frances Sawyer, Transition Finance): S&P downgraded Edison International and SCE as wildfire losses (Eaton, Camp) deplete the $21B wildfire fund to $14-16B; even under SB 254, utilities may need continuous multi-billion dollar buffers to stay investment-grade. #Edison #SCE #credit-rating #wildfire-fund #SB-254 #financial-risk

- [Pyrologix utilities solution](https://pyrologix.com/solutions/utilities/): Pyrologix Wildfire Risk (PWR) platform provides actionable risk analytics to utilities to prevent catastrophic wildfires and prioritize risk management; utilities could be a funder or revenue generator with insurance. #utilities #wildfire-risk #Pyrologix #risk-analytics #ignition

- *SB254 Earthquake Authority Wildfire Task Force* (Laurie Johnson, CEA): Presentation to the Dec 2025 WFRTF meeting on indemnity funds acting as risk pools for utilities, kicking in after roughly $1B/yr losses with shared 50/50 funding (ratepayers + utilities); notes a study exists to develop long-term fund solutions and equitable allocation. #SB-254 #indemnity-fund #risk-pool #utilities #Earthquake-Authority
