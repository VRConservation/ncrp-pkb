# Insurance Data


**Summary**: Risk data, catastrophe modeling, and the WUI Data Commons—the data infrastructure needed to inform wildfire mitigation, insurance, and risk modeling.
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01.

## WUI Data Commons

- [WUI Data Commons](https://www.milliman.com/en/video/wui-data-commons-wildfire-resilience-data-transparency): Milliman webinar introducing the WUI Data Commons, an initiative to collect and store wildfire mitigation and suppression data with controlled access; grant from Moore Foundation to the California Fire Chiefs Association; also a Little Hoover Commission slide deck on the same. #wui-data-commons #wildfire #data #mitigation #milliman

- [WUI Data Commons Phase II](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/millimaninc5660-milliman6442-prod27d5-0001/media/Milliman/PDFs/2025-Articles/7-18-25_WUI-Data-Commons-Phase-2-report.pdf): Milliman Phase 2 report on behalf of CalChiefs. Rationale centers on increasing data availability, transparency, and alignment; focuses on hard-to-obtain data, processes parcel- and community-level data, and calls for verified, inexpensive inspection technology to feed verified mitigation data to insurers. #wui-data-commons #wildfire #data #mitigation #alignment

- *Heather Kanzlemar WUI Data Commons 7-8-25*: Milliman actuarial consulting on community-based catastrophe insurance and sustainable insurance strategy. Notes data resolution isn't good enough and loss data isn't regularly collected; trend toward non-admitted (excess/surplus) market; wildfire losses are highly correlated for community-based insurance. #wui-data-commons #catastrophe-modeling #data #non-admitted #community-insurance

## Catastrophe modeling and risk data

- *Dan Montagnani 7-7-25*: Discussion of geospatial/data analytics firms (Geocerta, SimAnalytica, MapImpact) and catastrophe data for insurers/reinsurers. Swiss Re acquired Fathom (flood risk) at 500 m2 resolution—not great. Insurers must want to solve the problem; MapImpact wildfire data is landcover-driven, not physics/climate-driven. #data #geospatial #catastrophe-modeling #wildfire #reinsurance

- [Epicenter hazard briefing](https://www.epicenterinsights.com/welcome-to-the-epicenter/): Epicenter hazard briefing resource on climate and catastrophe risk; also has an insurance beat page aggregating insurance-related coverage. #hazard #risk #insurance #climate

## Wildfire data convening

- [CWI 2025 Convening Report](https://923d0f4a-ced5-4469-98f9-1a022ef01db3.usrfiles.com/ugd/923d0f_1977bbcd5349499a9809ed2346d9a0af.pdf): Wildfire data workshop focused on insurance and risk modeling. Key insight: we have much of the data we need; the gap is access, translation, alignment, and application. Calls for risk models beyond hazard mapping, trusted third-party intermediaries, open source models, scenario tools, and data commons interoperability. #wildfire #data #risk-modeling #insurance #workshop

- *Sarah Caldwell CWI 6-10-25*: Meeting report on the CWI convening (4th iteration), which started with an insurance/risk modeling panel including Rock Rose Risk and integrated risk modeling into FAIR Plan allocation pilots. Ties into the WUI Data Commons, Tahoe basin fire-smart projects, and a Coalition of Coalitions looking at data commons, wildfire intelligence, and the Western Wildfire Index, leading to a fall 2026 roundtable. #CWI #wildfire-data #Rock-Rose #risk-modeling #convening

## Homeowners insurance data

- [Homeowners Insurance Data Insights](https://doi.org/10.1111/rmir.70010): Gourevitch and Kousky assessment of the Treasury Federal Insurance Office's January 2025 Property and Casualty Market Intelligence (PCMI) dataset—246 million policies, 2018–2022, zip-code level. Highlights geographic regions with high losses/premiums and low availability, but no strong evidence of worsening trends over the short sample period. #homeowners-insurance #data #PCMI #premiums #availability
