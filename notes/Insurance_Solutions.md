# Insurance Solutions

## Proactive Insurance and Resilience

- [How Insurers Are Turning Risk Exposure into Resilience Advantage](https://www.aon.com/en/insights/articles/how-insurers-are-turning-risk-exposure-into-resilience-advantage?ref=epicenterinsights.com): Aon argues insurers must shift from reactive, post-loss mindsets to proactive strategies using forward-looking climate data, analytics, and product innovation. Introduces the Climate Resilience Maturity Curve to help insurers assess capabilities and drive profitable growth. #proactive #resilience #underwriting #climate_data #Aon

- [Encouraging Property Insurers to Invest in Communities](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/encouraging-property-insurers-to-invest-in-communities): Proposes tax incentives, risk-sharing mechanisms, and community investment requirements to prevent insurance deserts. Highlights success stories where insurers partnered with local governments on wildfire mitigation, resulting in lower claims and renewed market participation. #insurer_withdrawal #community_investment #wildfire #policy_reform #protection_gap

- [Unlocking Insurance to Rebuild Stronger and Greener after Disasters](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/unlocking-insurance-to-rebuild-stronger-and-greener-after-disasters): Proposes climate endorsements — optional riders on homeowners policies that provide additional funding for climate-friendly building materials, electrification, energy-efficient systems, or certified construction techniques during post-disaster rebuilding. #climate_endorsements #greener_rebuilding #electrification #IBHS #FORTIFIED

## Cost of Resilient Rebuilding

- [Resilience Delta: Designing Grants to Fund the Resilience Delta](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/resilience-delta): Defines the "resilience delta" as the extra cost of rebuilding resiliently versus typically. Wildfire-resistant construction adds roughly $30,000 for a 2,000 sq ft home; FORTIFIED roof upgrades add $1,000–$3,000. Reviews building codes, endorsements, grants, and loans as funding mechanisms. #resilience_delta #wildfire #FORTIFIED #grants #code_upgrade

- *In Altadena, Attempting to Rebuild for Resilience*: A Marketplace podcast exploring mass timber (CLT) construction in Altadena as a faster, more affordable way to rebuild after the Eaton Fire. CLT framing could enable five homes for the labor cost of one traditional home, while creating rural manufacturing jobs. #CLT #mass_timber #Altadena #affordable_rebuilding #Eaton_Fire

## Community Resilience Programs

- [California Resilience Challenge](https://resilientcal.org/): Features community-level resilience initiatives including Resilience Hubs — one-stop shops providing charging stations, medication refrigeration, and clean water after disasters. The Greenbelt Alliance works across nine Bay Area counties to manage greenbelts and mitigate wildfire risk in Sonoma County. #resilience_hub #Sonoma #Greenbelt_Alliance #wildfire #community_resilience

- [Protecting Homes and Building Trust: OneShoreline's Home Protection and Insurability Initiative](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/protecting-homes-and-building-trust-oneshorelines-home-protection-and-insurability-initiative): Details OneShoreline's microgrant program providing up to $1,500 for household-level flood mitigation measures like sump pumps and garage flood barriers. Part of the San Mateo County Flood and Sea Level Rise Resiliency District, a special district formed in 2019 to build long-term climate resilience. #OneShoreline #microgrants #flood_mitigation #San_Mateo #sea_level_rise
