# SLR


**Summary**: Insurance and finance strategies for building resilience to sea level rise, including community-based catastrophe insurance, resilience districts, performance-based risk transfer, and nature-based solutions.

## Insurance and Finance for Coastal Resilience

- *Insurance and Finance Strategies for Resilience to SLR — Humboldt Bay*: Explores how Wigi/Humboldt Bay can leverage insurance and climate financing for coastal resilience planning. Key opportunities include community-based catastrophe insurance, performance-based risk transfer using surety or impact bonds, and place-based insurance design developed locally with small companies. Key barriers include conflicting timelines between annual insurance cycles and long-term adaptation projects, limited access to risk data, and science gaps on the risk reduction effectiveness of nature-based solutions. #Humboldt_Bay #sea_level_rise #community_catastrophe_insurance #nature_based_solutions #parametric

- *Mitigation Discounts and Community-Level Incentives*: Current mitigation discounts for individual home hardening are small and often depend on neighbors also fortifying. Community-level mitigation incentives — where collective action across a neighborhood reduces aggregate risk — could be more transformative in lowering premiums and encouraging coastal resilience. #mitigation_discounts #premium_reduction #community_level

## Resilience Districts and Financing Mechanisms

- *Resilience Districts as Insurance Mechanisms*: Proposes resilience districts where landowners tax themselves to create maintenance funds, enabling community-level self-insurance pools that lower individual premiums. Communities could bring pooled resources to insurers to develop boutique plans incentivizing nature-based solutions and risk reduction. #resilience_district #self_insurance #flood #community_deductible

- *Tax Increment Financing for Adaptation Infrastructure*: Describes using tax incremental financing — where infrastructure investments raise surrounding property values — to fund adaptation projects. The incremental tax gains repay the investment over time while also increasing property values. #TIF #infrastructure_funding #adaptation

- *Agriculture Model for Rolling Easements*: Explores rolling easements as a mechanism for managing coastal land use transitions, potentially applicable to sea level rise adaptation and managed retreat. #rolling_easements #agriculture #land_use_transition

## Nature-Based Solutions and Measurement

- *Monitoring Nature-Based Solutions for Insurance*: Living seawalls and other pilot projects face challenges in proving risk reduction, especially after large storms like those in 2020 where some pilots failed. Drawing on multiple data types — including observational and co-benefit data on tourism, health, and community well-being — is critical for making the case to insurers and regulators. #NBS #monitoring #living_seawalls #data #co_benefits

- *Monetizing Nature-Based Solutions*: The insurance industry thinks in terms of capital preservation rather than returns, making it difficult to justify NBS investments. Key questions include how to quantify risk reduction benefits, whether carbon credit markets can provide a revenue stream, and how cost-avoidance instruments could be built around avoided damages. #monetization #carbon_credits #cost_avoidance #capital_stack
