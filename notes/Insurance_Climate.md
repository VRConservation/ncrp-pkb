# Insurance Climate


**Summary**: Examines how climate change is reshaping the insurance industry, shrinking the range of insurability, and driving innovation in risk transfer, resilience building, and climate disclosure.
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01.

---

## The Insurability Crisis

- [Is the future insurable? Global climate change is shrinking the range of insurability](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/629aa6f68914ce47a1d72993/t/689d3c2fbb79ac401be73a8d/1755135023586/Is+the+future+insurable_+2025.pdf#page=4.06): Explores how climate change challenges property insurance through technical, economic, and political limits, narrowing the window of insurability. #insurability #climate-risk #parametric-insurance #risk-transfer #adaptation

- [NRDC: An Uninsurable Country](https://www.nrdc.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/Insurability_and_CC_R_26-02-A_04_locked.pdf): Argues that the built environment was designed for past risks and is increasingly uninsurable as climate hazards mount, with 13.6 million U.S. homes uninsured. #insurability #climate-risk #FAIR-Plan #uninsured-homes #building-codes

- [Even Without Hurricanes, U.S. Disaster Costs Surpassed $100 Billion Last Year](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/01/08/climate/us-disaster-damage-costs-2025.html): In 2025, thunderstorms and the LA wildfires drove U.S. disaster costs above $100 billion for the fifth time in six years, without a single hurricane strike. #disaster-costs #billion-dollar-disasters #climate-disaster #thunderstorms

- [Destroy, Rebuild, Repeat](https://scholarship.law.vanderbilt.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=5187&context=vlr): Argues that climate and disaster law has not kept pace with climate change, creating a reactive "destroy, rebuild, repeat" cycle that traps communities. #disaster-law #managed-retreat #flood-disclosure #climate-adaptation

## Insurance as a Resilience Tool

- [Can insurance save us from climate disaster](https://vitalsigns.edf.org/story/can-insurance-save-us-climate-disaster): Examines parametric insurance as a new model for faster payouts and damage prevention, but notes insurance alone cannot solve climate risk without broader risk reduction. #parametric-insurance #climate-resilience #wetlands-protection

- [Building Resilience Through Insurance](https://www.climateproof.news/p/carolyn-kousky-on-building-resilience-through-insurance): Carolyn Kousky discusses Insurance for Good's mission to improve equity in disaster recovery, invest in resilience, and the promise of parametric insurance for filling protection gaps. #insurance-for-good #parametric-insurance #disaster-recovery #resilience

- [Building Resilience Carolyn Kousky Transcript Summary pt. 1 and 2](https://www.climateproof.news/p/carolyn-kousky-on-building-resilience-through-insurance): Detailed breakdown of Kousky's views on nature-based solutions for managed retreat, the limits of insurance for chronic risks, and behavioral economics in post-disaster decision-making. #nature-based-solutions #managed-retreat #uninsurability #behavioral-economics

- [Building a climate resilient future](https://www.oliverwyman.com/our-expertise/insights/2023/dec/how-the-insurance-industry-can-build-climate-resilience.html): Oliver Wyman report from COP28 outlining five priorities for insurers: resilience strategy, public-private partnerships, better data, build-back-better standards, and policy advocacy. #climate-resilience #public-private-partnerships #build-back-better #COP28

## Risk Reduction and Linking Insurance to Resilience

- [5 ways to better link risk reduction and insurance](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/5-ways-to-better-link-risk-reduction-and-insurance): Kousky and Key outline five principles for aligning insurance with risk reduction, including coordinated incentives across sectors and insurer support for resilience financing. #risk-reduction #insurance-alignment #resilience-financing #building-codes

- *Risk, Return, Resilience (Josh Harrison)*: Proposes a four-pillar framework to transform insurance from a reactive risk-pricer into a proactive climate adaptation partner, covering capital reallocation, product innovation, governance reform, and equity integration. #insurance-reform #climate-adaptation #equity #parametric-insurance

- [Podcast: Innovations in Climate Risk and Insurance](https://www.americaadapts.org/episodes/innovations-in-climate-risk-and-insurance-the-podcast): America Adapts podcast featuring discussions on adaptation mindset, insurance as a tool for risk transfer, wildfire as a frontline risk, and blending philanthropy with insurance innovation. #insurance-innovation #wildfire #adaptation #climate-risk

## State Residual Markets and FAIR Plans

- [Future of State-Created Insurance Programmes](https://journalofcrr.com/comment/03-01-kousky-friedrich/): Kousky and Friedrich overview state residual insurance markets (FAIR plans, Citizens), their growth, hidden cross-subsidies, and the tension between affordability and actuarial soundness. #FAIR-Plan #residual-markets #Citizens-Insurance #insurance-affordability

## Climate Disclosure and Transparency

- [Ceres report: Major U.S. Insurers Make Progress on Climate Disclosures, but Significant Gaps Remain](https://www.ceres.org/resources/news/major-us-insurers-make-progress-on-climate-disclosures-but-significant-gaps-remain-2025): Finds 99% of insurers report on risk management but only 29% disclose climate metrics and targets, with the metrics-and-targets pillar lagging significantly. #climate-disclosure #TCFD #insurance-transparency #climate-scenario-analysis
