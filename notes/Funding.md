## Municipal bonds

- [Resilience at scale means the muni bond market](https://cafwd.org/news/resilience-at-scale-means-the-municipal-bond-market/): Third in a CA FWD/Insurance for Good series. Muni bond market is the backbone; is deep, liquid, and built for long-lived public assets. If 10% of annual issuance aligned with resilience, that's ~$50B/year. #municipal-bonds #financing #resilience #funding #scalability

- [Financing pathways incl muni bonds](https://tapin.waternow.org/wildfire-resilience-financing-pathways/): WaterNow analysis of whether public water providers can use municipal revenue bond proceeds for nature-based watershed and wildfire resilience projects; Colorado permits it with case studies from Greeley and Denver Water. #municipal-bonds #water #watershed #wildfire #financing

- *Muni bond articles*: Collection of muni bond examples, including Flagstaff's voter-approved municipal bond partnering city, Coconino National Forest, and Arizona to reduce wildfire and post-fire flooding risk without increasing property taxes; also links to WaterNow pathways and CA FWD muni bond article. #municipal-bonds #Flagstaff #wildfire #flooding #financing

## Funding and financing options

- [Funding and financing options for resilience investments](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/funding-and-financing-for-resilience): First installment of a CA FWD and Insurance for Good blog series; distinguishes funding vs. financing and lays out types of funding and financing options for risk reduction and resilience. #funding #financing #resilience #risk-reduction

- [Risk ownership in a time of crisis](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/cafwd-i4g2): Second installment in the CA FWD/Insurance for Good series. Emphasizes valuing risk reduction, bridging physical/financial risk perspectives (Hoboken resilience park example), limits of insurance capital alone for mitigation, and making risk ownership shared—not abandoned—via public/private funding books, resilience districts, coalitions. #risk-ownership #resilience #financing #funding #insurance-capital

## Investment returns and cost-effectiveness

- [WRI investment and disaster](https://www.wri.org/research/climate-adaptation-investment-case): WRI research across 12 countries shows US$1 invested in adaptation yields over $10.50 in benefits over 10 years ($133B invested generating $1.4T), with typical returns of 20–27%. Uses triple dividend framework—avoided losses, economic benefits, social/environmental gains. #adaptation #investment #cost-benefit #triple-dividend #returns

- [Resilience investment](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/blog/california-cant-afford-to-wait): Climate risk repricing the $4T U.S. municipal bond market and California's FAIR Plan liability reaching $724B. California's $3.7B Climate Resilience Package is hard to sustain given budget structure; long-term risk reduction competes with immediate service needs. #climaterisk #munibonds #FAIR-Plan #budget #resilience

## What is parametric insurance

- [What is Parametric Insurance](https://corporatesolutions.swissre.com/insights/knowledge/what_is_parametric_insurance.html): Parametric (index-based) insurance covers the probability of a loss-causing event rather than indemnifying actual losses, paying out when a pre-defined intensity threshold is met as measured by an objective parameter. #parametric #index-based #threshold #objective-trigger #payout

## Parametric insurance in practice

- *Capitol Gains podcast — Climate, Insurance and Risk Management with Carolyn Kousky*: Parametric insurance pays out automatically based on triggers like wind speed or rainfall, offering fast cash, flexibility for non-property losses, and accessibility for renters and small businesses; however it must be paired with broader policy reforms and resilient building codes to be effective. [[Insurance]] [[Climate]] #parametric #Carolyn-Kousky #policy-reform #building-codes #resilience #equity

- *Tahoe Donner Wildfire Resilience Insurance*: A parametric policy piloted in Truckee, CA pays a predetermined amount per burned acre across 1,345 insured acres (up to $2.5M), with lower payouts for beneficial low-severity fire; forest treatment resulted in a 39% lower premium and 84% lower deductible, demonstrating nature-based risk reduction can be recognized by insurers. [[Fire]] [[NBS]] #wildfire #parametric #Tahoe-Donner #forest-management #prescribed-fire #satellite-imagery

- [Floodbase — Parametric Flood Insurance](https://www.floodbase.com/): Floodbase pioneered large-area flood mapping technology that enables a new category of parametric flood insurance, paying out within days based on the magnitude of flooding over a wide area rather than individual property assessments. [[Flooding]] #flood #parametric #flood-mapping #rapid-payout #large-area
