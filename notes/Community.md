# Community


**Summary**: Community-based catastrophe insurance (CBCI), group or meso-insurance, and insurance innovation that meet the social and equity needs of low- and moderate-income households.
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01.

## Group and Community-Based Insurance Models

- [Group Insurance](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/group-insurance): New insurance models use an intermediary institution between insurer and policyholder to secure coverage for a group; forms include *aggregator* (meso-insurance) and *community insurance* (when a local government or public entity is the intermediary). #aggregator #meso-insurance #community-insurance #intermediary

- *Community Based Catastrophe Insurance (CBCI)*: A disaster insurance program arranged by a local government, quasi-governmental body (e.g., special-purpose district), or community group covering individual properties within a community; its structure is highly flexible and tailorable to different communities and needs. #CBCI #special-district #local-government #community-coverage

- *Embedded insurance models*: Policies could be embedded with other products or accompany participation in programs, offering a way to deliver [microinsurance](https://www.insuranceforgood.org/microinsurance) policies and raising them to the level of an aggregator model. #embedded-insurance #microinsurance #aggregator

## Parametric Rainfall Risk Transfer Pilot (NYC)

- [Meso insurance pilot in NYC](https://www.edf.org/sites/default/files/2023-11/Harnessing%20Risk%20Transfer%20Report%20Final.pdf): A meso-insurance pilot addressing the delays low- and moderate-income households face receiving funds for immediate post-disaster needs; a cross-sectoral team designed a parametric rainfall risk transfer product purchased by the Center for NYC Neighborhoods to fund emergency cash grants after qualifying floods. #parametric #meso-insurance #rainfall-flood #cash-grants #low-income

- [NY nonprofit pilots community-based flood insurance](https://www.marketplace.org/story/2024/10/23/ny-nonprofit-is-piloting-community-based-flood-insurance-could-it-be-a-model): Parametric payout is fast because there is no loss adjustment; money arrives without waiting for an insurer to inspect damage, payout is automatic, and can be used for whatever the family needs most. #parametric #fast-payout #community-flood-insurance #flexibility

- [Inclusive insurance](https://www.edf.org/inclusive-insurance): More on the 'inclusive' insurance work linked to the NYC parametric rainfall pilot. #inclusive-insurance #parametric

- *Harnessing Risk Transfer to Support Immediate Post-Disaster Needs of LMI Households* (Kousky, Wiley, et al., October 2023): Documents the NYC meso-insurance pilot that uses parametric risk transfer to deliver fast, flexible $5K–$10K cash grants to LMI households (income ≤165% area median) after floods, combining a Swiss Re parametric policy (brokered by Guy Carpenter) with blended data (satellite, ground sensors, social media) and a streamlined community-led application portal. #parametric #meso-insurance #LMI #cash-grants #implementation-lessons
  - Lessons for community organizations: build cross-sector teams, define target gaps (timing/peril/population), ensure compatibility with FEMA, iterate on trigger design, secure board approval early, minimize paperwork, and plan for fraud prevention and tech infrastructure.
  - Challenges: parametric products don't fit traditional insurance frameworks (pilot used a derivative structure); premiums need long-term philanthropic/government funding; rainfall flood triggers lack ideal real-time metrics.

## Insurance Innovation to Meet Social Needs

- *Community-Driven and Research-Informed: Insurance Innovation to Meet Social Needs* (Kousky & Wiley, November 2023): Outlines a five-step process — assessing post-disaster financial needs, scanning interventions, engaging stakeholders, refining design, and preparing for pilot evaluation — to develop equitable insurance solutions for LMI households after climate disasters, focused on NYC rainfall flooding. #innovation #community-driven #research-informed #recovery-needs #LMI
  - Landscape scanning considered six interventions: parametric microinsurance, premium reductions for low-cost mitigation, local flood-insurance subsidies, community assistance paired with high-deductible NFIP policies, community-based insurance, and right-sizing coverage via expert consultations.
  - Stakeholder engagement (30+ interviews and focus groups) narrowed the focus to fast, flexible cash grants funded by a parametric policy purchased by the Center for NYC Neighborhoods, with private partners (Guy Carpenter, Swiss Re) designing the trigger and terms.
