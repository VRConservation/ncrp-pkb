# Insurance Nature


**Summary**: Explores the two-way relationship between insurance markets and natural ecosystems — how nature-based solutions reduce insured losses, and how the insurance sector can drive conservation and restoration.
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01.

---

## Nature for Insurance: How Ecosystems Support Insurance Markets

- [Nature for Insurance and Insurance for Nature](https://library.edf.org/AssetLink/uky3347ks0532vxm44um360jb0x2uwup.pdf#page=3.60): EDF report on the two-way relationship between insurance and nature — healthy ecosystems reduce disaster risk and stabilize insurance markets, while insurance tools can drive conservation. Identifies five levers: new products, new business models, nature in underwriting, nature in investing, and insuring nature itself. #insurance_solutions #nature_based_solutions #biodiversity #insurance_markets #conservation #risk_reduction

- *Nature Benefits — NBS Cost Advantage*: Nature-based solutions are 6x cheaper than post-disaster spending, but they are small, scattered, and slow to deploy. #nature_based_solutions #cost_effectiveness #disaster_spending #resilience

- [Cost-effectiveness of Nature-Based Solutions for Disaster Risk Reduction](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0048969724046722?via%3Dihub): Review of 2000–2021 studies found 71% of NbS consistently cost-effective for mitigating hazards; 80% effective for mangroves, 77% for forests, 73% for coastal ecosystems. NbS were more cost-effective than engineering solutions in 65% of comparative studies. #nature_based_solutions #cost_effectiveness #mangroves #forests #disaster_risk_reduction #hazard_mitigation

- [Nature's Remedy: Improving Flood Resilience Through Insurance and Nature-Based Mitigation](https://www.nature.org/content/dam/tnc/nature/en/documents/ImprovingFloodResilienceThroughInsuranceandNatureBasedMitigation_21NOV01.pdf#page=4.09): TNC analysis showing how incorporating risk reduction from nature-based solutions (e.g., levee setbacks) into NFIP pricing could lower premiums for protected homeowners. #flood_insurance #nature_based_solutions #NFIP #levee_setbacks #flood_resilience #premium_reduction

## Green Infrastructure and Flood Risk

- [The Surprising Limits of Flood Infrastructure](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-60267-6): Study of 138 capital cities found that grey drainage infrastructure loses effectiveness under extreme rainfall (>200mm/day). Green spaces become the most significant flood reduction factor during extreme events — a 1% increase in green space cuts flooding by 0.16% vs. 0.02% under normal rainfall. #green_infrastructure #flood_risk #urban_resilience #extreme_rainfall #nature_based_solutions #climate_adaptation

- [Scientific Evidence for Ecosystem-Based Disaster Risk Reduction](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41893-021-00732-4#citeas): Review of 529 articles (2000–2019) demonstrates robust links between ecosystems and hazard reduction, particularly vegetation stabilizing steep slopes. Research gaps remain in coastal, dryland, and watershed areas, especially in the Global South. #ecosystem_services #disaster_risk_reduction #evidence_review #vegetation #slope_stabilization #global_south
