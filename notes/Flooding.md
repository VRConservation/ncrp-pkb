## National Flood Insurance Program

- *Community based insurance options*: Overview of the FEMA NFIP, established 1968, with about 5.4 million individual policies insuring roughly $1.3 trillion of property, 80% at risk-based rates. Challenges include large program debt, low purchase rates in flood-prone areas, affordability issues, and severe repetitive-loss properties. Discusses a community-based flood insurance option and key questions for consideration from the 2015 FEMA-requested report. #NFIP #FEMA #flood-insurance #community-insurance #affordability

- *National Flood Insurance Program*: The federal government is the primary flood insurer in the U.S. via the NFIP, so taxpayers foot the bill for massive claims. Under Biden, FEMA was required to invest in risk management and increase household cost share; Mississippi senators now urge reversing "Risk Rating 2.0" pricing citing premium spikes. Argues risk-reflective pricing, avoiding underpriced risk, and keeping people insured are critical for fiscal sustainability. #NFIP #Risk-Rating-2.0 #risk-pricing #taxpayers #insurance/flood

- [Insurance canary in the coal mine](https://www.epicenterinsights.com/the-weekly-the-insurance-canary-in-the-coal-mine/): When the federal government shut down, the NFIP also lapsed. A lapsed NFIP can halt real estate transactions where mortgages require flood insurance, since lenders cannot issue government-backed mortgages in special flood hazard zones without coverage that many private insurers won't provide. #NFIP #government-shutdown #lenders #mortgage #flood-hazard

## Community-based flood insurance

- [Flood Risk Rising: Can Community-Based Insurance Fill a Gap?](https://www.ucdavis.edu/climate/news/flood-risk-rising-can-community-based-insurance-fill-gap): Covers the Isleton, CA community-based flood insurance pilot and its benefits—quick financial recovery, equity for all property owners, reduced reliance on federal aid, and proactive flood management. Notes that lower-income households are least likely to have flood insurance and slowest to access aid; a single policy bought by a community intermediary can trigger predefined payments (e.g. $10,000) at flood. California paid $3 billion more into the NFIP than it received (1994–2015), motivating state flood insurance alternatives. #community-insurance #Isleton #parametric #equity #NFIP #insurance/flood

- [A Proposed Design for Community Flood Insurance](https://www.rff.org/publications/reports/a-proposed-design-for-community-flood-insurance/): Kousky and Shabman (2015, RFF) propose a community flood insurance policy: a single policy purchased by a local governmental or quasi-governmental body covering a group of properties. Drawing on parametric insurance principles can cut costs, with savings passed to the community; implementation requires engagement by both communities and insurers. #community-insurance #parametric #policy-design #RFF #flood-insurance

- *Community based insurance options*: 2015 FEMA-requested report identifying key issues for a community-based flood insurance program. Community-based options could provide coverage for all or nearly all at-risk residents, though many current NFIP challenges (debt, low uptake, affordability) may not be resolved by this approach. #NFIP #community-based #FEMA #flood-insurance

## Parametric and innovative flood insurance

- [Floodbase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UFL9YfCKCwc): Floodbase pioneered flood mapping technology that expands brokers' addressable risk and powers a new category of parametric flood insurance. Products pay out based on the magnitude of flooding over a large area, within days of an event, letting the insured use proceeds at their discretion; combines science with an assembled carrier panel for large-area flood products. See [floodbase.com](https://www.floodbase.com/). #parametric #flood-mapping #floodbase #insurance/flood

## Managed Retreat and Community Resilience

- [Small Towns, Big Flood: Managed Retreat in Two Midwestern Towns](https://www.ucdavis.edu/climate/news/small-towns-big-flood-waters): Managed retreat experiences in small Midwestern towns (one with 900 residents) highlight the need for strong community engagement and early planning before floodwaters arrive. #managed_retreat #community_resilience #flood_planning #midwest #small_towns

- [Unbuilding the Terrace](https://99percentinvisible.org/episode/nbft-04-unbuilding-the-terrace/): From the "Not Built for This" podcast—covers managed retreat and buyouts, including the Valmeyer IL 1993 flood example and Lake Charles, Louisiana's Greinwich Terrace. Notes there's not enough money to build out of flood risk, buyouts often target disadvantaged communities (redlining, low-lying cheap land), and FEMA has bought out 43,000 properties in the last 3 years. Highlights equity concerns behind managed retreat. #managed-retreat #buyouts #equity #FEMA #Not-Built-For-This #flood

- [The Price Is Wrong (Not Built for This series)](https://99percentinvisible.org/episode/nbft-03-the-price-is-wrong/): Explores Cape Coral, Florida, developed in the 1950s as a canal-based waterfront paradise, where nearly 90% of properties are now at flood risk. Notes insurers know more about climate risk than anyone, FEMA underprices risk (Risk Rating 2.0 brought a 250% increase in Florida flood premiums, with rates jumping from $500 to $7,000 around Hurricane Ian), and the wealthy can self insure while people of means buy in after disasters. #Cape-Coral #Risk-Rating-2.0 #premiums #climate-risk #self-insurance #flood

## Research and equity

- [Improving the Post-Flood Financial Resilience of Lower-Income Households through Insurance](https://www.edf.org/sites/default/files/2022-09/Improving-LMI-Household-Flood-Insurance-Options_Issue-Brief.pdf): Kousky and Wiley (2021, EDF) brief for policymakers on the critical role insurance can play in securing LMI household resilience. Explores six policy options: parametric microinsurance, premium reductions for low-cost mitigation, local affordability programs, community assistance with high-deductible NFIP, community-based insurance, and right-sizing coverage. #equity #LMI #financial-resilience #parametric #community-insurance #flood-insurance

- [Vehicle flood damage and household disaster assistance in the United States](https://doi.org/10.1111/rmir.70002): Koller (2025) first analysis of FEMA IHP Transportation Assistance data for vehicle flood damages (2007–2022). FEMA awarded over $160 million for uninsured vehicle flood damages; more than half of applicants were renters and nearly two-thirds had incomes of $30,000 or less. Identifies insurance coverage gaps, risk-tolerant behavior, and limited federal support as drivers of vehicle-owner flood vulnerability. #vehicles #disaster-assistance #FEMA #vulnerability #equity #flood-damage
