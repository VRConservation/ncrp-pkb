# Flooding


**Summary**: Covers flood risk, flood disaster planning, managed retreat, and evacuation strategies, with emphasis on community-level preparedness and the limits of traditional flood infrastructure.
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01.

## Flood Infrastructure and Risk

- *The Surprising Limits of Flood Infrastructure*: Cross-reference — key study showing grey infrastructure loses effectiveness under extreme rainfall, while green infrastructure becomes critical. See full entry on [[Insurance_Nature]]. #flood_risk #green_infrastructure #extreme_rainfall

- *Scientific Evidence for Ecosystem-Based Disaster Risk Reduction*: Cross-reference — review of 529 articles establishing robust links between ecosystems and hazard reduction. See full entry on [[Insurance_Nature]]. #ecosystem_services #disaster_risk_reduction #evidence_review

## Flood Evacuation Planning

- [Designing RoutScape: Geospatial Prototyping with XR for Flood Evacuation Planning](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.06045): XR prototype for visualizing flood scenarios and evacuation routes, developed with DRRM officers in the Philippines. Features flood depth visualization, route planning, and multi-user collaboration on Meta Quest headsets. #extended_reality #flood_evacuation #disaster_risk_reduction #collaborative_planning #geospatial_visualization

## Managed Retreat and Community Resilience

- [Small Towns, Big Flood: Managed Retreat in Two Midwestern Towns](https://www.ucdavis.edu/climate/news/small-towns-big-flood-waters): Managed retreat experiences in small Midwestern towns (one with 900 residents) highlight the need for strong community engagement and early planning before floodwaters arrive. #managed_retreat #community_resilience #flood_planning #midwest #small_towns
